27stack is an open foundation for running your own cloud, with services you install and update like apps. It's the working piece of a larger plan: right now, governments and most other organizations rent their software from companies that lock them in and charge for every change. Instead, we can build open source tools that states and cities own and share with each other, starting small and local, and building the capacity to work at the federal level by 2029. [The plan](/plan) lays out how.
